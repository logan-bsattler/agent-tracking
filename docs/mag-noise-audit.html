MAG noise audit

Read-only audit of MAG board output and MAG/work/ files, 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-17 (14 days). Board read with sqlite3 …coord.db?mode=ro. No code changed.

Goal (Ben, 2026-11-17): more work reaching Done, meaning promoted or drafted. Versions are fine; the problem is chains that never finish. In this window 5 of 87 MAG deliverable tasks were promoted. Work stalls between "written" and "approved": 55% of reviews ask for changes, approvals' minor notes are turned into new tasks, parallel revisions leave no single candidate, and conditional tasks keep chains open. Section 3 ranks the fixes by how much more work they get to Done.

Bottom line: about 3% of MAG's board output reached a client or Ben. About 55% is superseded drafts or agent-to-agent process. Of 301 tasks (1.28 MB, about 320k tokens of specs, results, decisions, loose ends and parks), 17 tasks (54 KB) ended as an Outlook draft or a promoted deliverable. 77 tasks (334 KB) were superseded, duplicated, failed or never read. 70 tasks (379 KB) were review rounds and process material. The work/ folder grew by 699 files (45 MB), and 37% of those bytes are older versions of files that were later replaced.

The biggest source is new, and it has no brakes: the Ben-direct hand-back (direct.autotask, added 2026-11-16). In about 24 hours, one direct ask (9fcb50eca2a3) grew into a 32-task chain. Every loose end became its own task, often a deliverable that then got its own automatic review, which raised more loose ends. That path skips the two-round review cap and turns conditional notes ("When Ben closes …") into open tasks. Fixing autotask is the cheapest large win and needs no schema change.

The workflow engine is not a MAG noise source. It created 0 MAG tasks and 0 interim replies in this window.

1a. DoD reviews: reviews are larger than the work they review

  • An average review result is 4.3 KB. An average deliverable result is 2.6 KB. Review criteria alone total 146 KB, and deliverable self_check another 128 KB. Both restate the same checklist item by item.
  • 55% of reviews request changes. 8 of 32 deliverable chains went through 3 or more versions. The worst: the mapping-confidence page (c85b6126), with 16 deliverables and 7 reviews; rejected-browse FDDs (4faac79a), with 13 and 5; the wave-plan re-cut (801c7985), with 6 and 5.
  • Only 5 MAG deliverable tasks were promoted in the window (20 files). All the other review effort went into versions that were superseded or never shipped.
  • Each review runs in a fresh or cleared session (about 90-120k cache write, per the 2026-11-02 token note). The cost per review is far larger than its board bytes.

1b. Ben-direct hand-back: six defects in direct.autotask

  1. One task per loose end. groups = [fus] if revision else [[f] for f in fus] (direct.py ~229). A result with 4 client follow-ups spawns 4 tasks.
  2. The source's kind is inherited. _pick_kind returns deliverable when the source was a deliverable (25 of 36). Each child therefore gets an automatic dod_review, whose loose ends are tasked in turn. The fan-out is recursive.
  3. Conditional and status notes are tasked. 8 children are "When Ben closes a146…", "After Ben rules 49eb…", "Once the CSVs are saved", "On reply, fill…", "Sister task 4f8e…: run…", and "Task 79ae… is now done by this task". Five of these sit open and can't run. One (22fa95e6) failed.
  4. Nits from approving reviews become tasks. 4 children came from approve verdicts (62758894, cdfc8994, 06811fa0, ad620e52). The contract lets an approval carry non-blocker findings (contracts.py 185-190), so each nit turns into a task.
  5. No round cap. autotask never calls store.review_rounds. It opened a third-round revision twice: 750dcdd8 "Revise: Revise: Finish r11" and 4b64f896 "Revise: Revise: Revise 13 browse FDDs". review_owed then correctly declines a review, so these are revisions no one will check.
  6. Parallel revisions of one deliverable. 4faac79a has three revising children (bae100b7, 9bba7c31, 316021f3). c85b6126 has three (48620bba, 19e10ee5, 9d4a0c45). The autotask revision and a filed or master revision both run.
