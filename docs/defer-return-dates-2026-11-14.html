Defer return dates

agent-tracking commit ab359a8, 2026-11-14. A deferred item now comes back on a date. The workflow engine now drafts a reply even when its reply loose end is deferred.

What Ben must decide
  1. Run the backfill? 420 deferred items have no return date, 107 of them Ben's. The dry run spreads them at 42 a day over the next 10 business days, Ben's first: his 107 come back Nov 16 to 18, the clients' 313 from Nov 18 to 27. To turn it on: cd C:\development\agents\agent-tracking, then python -m coord_mcp.defer backfill --apply. Until then they stay parked as before.
  2. Holidays. Business days skip weekends only, so Nov 26 (Thanksgiving week) gets its 42. Re-run with --days 9 if that matters.
  3. Expired items come back once. An item the 14-day expiry defers now returns 10 business days later. It is then never expired again, under the existing rule, so it stays on the queue until someone acts on it. Say if you would rather it expire again.

Switches and commands

  • defer_return (on): the dashboard's reopen pass. Dates are still recorded when it is off. To turn it off: python -m coord_mcp.queue switch defer_return off.
  • python -m coord_mcp.defer backfill [--apply] [--days N], defer due (dry run of what would reopen now), defer list.
