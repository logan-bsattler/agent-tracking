Holds behind Ben: build summary

2026-11-14 · coord-mcp · the queue audit's sixth item · report: ben-holds-defaults-2026-11-14.html (+ .xlsx)

The rule is live for new holds; nothing on the board has changed. From now on, internal work behind a Ben question that has a default proceeds on that default, and his question stays in his queue. Outward steps (Outlook, check-in) wait for his answer. Of the 149 existing live holds behind Ben, 33 would proceed on a default. That apply is off until Ben reads the report and turns it on.

Not live: the existing holds
