How the pipeline works (map)

StepDetail
ScheduleThe hourly-triage desktop scheduled task runs at about :08 every hour, 07:00–18:00 CT, on weekdays. Each run is a fresh session in C:\development\agents.
Readoutlook_email_search over the whole mailbox (no folder filter), from afterDateTime = state.last_email_check, 25 per page, paging until done. Sent mail is read separately, for commitments and closing evidence only.
FilterIds already in seen_email_ids (last 500) are skipped. Newsletters, notifications and automated mail are counted on one line, not itemised.
ClientMatched by domain, signature, subject or pipeline item against 13 client keys. Anything else is unknown.
ClassifyEach email gets one of five outcomes:
  • email: work.
  • email-status: an update ask.
  • email-reply: only Ben can answer.
  • email-closes: evidence that a loose end is done.
  • A note line, otherwise.
Sent mail produces email-commitment. Duplicates are checked against open intents only.
Hand-offcoord_propose_intent writes the intent. Then send_message wakes local_198c7d6a-…, only when intents were proposed. The minder backs this up: it flags open intents after its threshold.
Statestate.json is written only after a successful run, so a failed run is re-covered by the next one.
