How a hold was read

  • A hold behind Ben: a standing (not superseded) decision that holds, defers, parks or keeps work waiting, whose trigger ("until …", "pending …", "waits on …") names Ben, his loose end or a question presented to him. Handoff snapshots are skipped.
  • Live: it still holds something open: a loose end it deferred, an open loose end or undelivered task it names, or its own task.
  • Default: the recommended option of the question presented on his loose end; else a default, recommendation or proposal named in his loose end; else one named in the hold. A "master default" in a hold's reason marks the hold as master's own call, not an answer to the question, so it is counted separately and is not used as the default.
  • Outward: the held work is a check-in, or names a send, Outlook, Promote, SharePoint, a tracker post or the client; or the hold says outward-facing. A draft reply is internal (its body is a file); its Outlook step is gated until Ben answers.
